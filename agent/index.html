Brand Manager Agent

It checks your inventory, picks out the products that need a promotional push, writes the post for you, and asks for your OK before anything goes live.

Try it with sample products

How it works

The loop the agent runs on every check, in plain English.

  1. 1

    Check the shelves

    Once a day, the agent looks at how much of each product is left in stock.

  2. 2

    Spot the slow movers

    It compares stock levels to how fast each product is actually selling, and flags anything piling up.

  3. 3

    Write the post

    For each flagged product, it drafts a short, ready-to-publish marketing message.

  4. 4

    Ask you first

    Nothing goes out without your OK. The draft is sent to your #marketing-approvals Slack channel and waits there for your reply.

  5. 5

    Publish it

    Once you approve, the post is scheduled through Buffer. No copy-pasting into another app.
