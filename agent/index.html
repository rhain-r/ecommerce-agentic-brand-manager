How it works

Five steps, no surprises

Nothing here is a black box — here's the entire loop the agent runs, in plain English.

1

Check the shelves

Every few hours, the agent looks at how much of each product is left in stock.

2

Spot the slow movers

It compares stock levels to how fast each product is actually selling, and flags anything piling up.

3

Write the post

For each flagged product, it drafts a short, ready-to-publish marketing message.

4

Ask you first

Nothing goes out without your OK — the draft lands in your approval queue, just like a Slack message.

5

Publish it

Once you approve, the post is scheduled automatically. No copy-pasting into another app.
